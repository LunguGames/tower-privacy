Privacy Policy of the game "Tower: Cyborg vs Zombies"

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the mobile game "Tower: Cyborg vs Zombies" (the Game), why, and how you can control it.

The developer of the Game (the Developer) is responsible for data processing in the Game. Contact: lungugamedev@gmail.com

1. Data that stays on your device

The Game stores your progress, settings (language, volume, vibration) and game statistics on your device. These records themselves are not sent to the Developer or to third parties; anonymous Game statistics are collected separately, as described in section 2.

The Game has no registration or accounts, does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and takes no payments. The Game may ask for permission to send notifications to remind you about rewards; this permission gives it no access to your data.

2. Data processed to show ads and for Game statistics

The Game has rewarded ads only: an ad is shown when you tap a button yourself to get a reward (for example, a second life or crystals). There are no other ads in the Game.

Ads are served by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK of YANDEX LLC. It includes the AppMetrica library (YANDEX LLC), which counts ad impressions. These services may process:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • device details: model, manufacturer, Android version, language, screen size, time zone;
  • network details: connection type, carrier, IP address (which may reveal your approximate country and city);
  • Game details: package name and version;
  • ad details: which ad was shown, whether it was watched to the end, whether it was tapped.

The Developer also uses AppMetrica to get anonymous Game statistics: Game launches and time spent in it, errors in its work, and game events — the start and end of a run and the wave reached, perks chosen and upgrades bought, rewards taken, taps on ad buttons, tutorial steps and opening the Game from a reminder. The device and network details listed above are sent with these events. The Developer sees these statistics only in AppMetrica's summary reports.

The Developer does not receive this data in a form that identifies you and does not store it.

3. Purposes

  • showing a rewarded ad when you choose to watch one;
  • choosing ads, including by your interests where the law of your country and your device settings allow it;
  • counting impressions and giving the reward in the Game;
  • protection against fraud and invalid ad traffic;
  • understanding how the Game is played: tuning its balance and fixing errors.

4. Sharing with third parties

The data listed in section 2 is processed by YANDEX LLC under its privacy policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

The Developer does not sell data and does not share it with anyone else.

5. Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete the Game or reset your progress in the Game's settings. Data processed by YANDEX LLC is kept for the periods set in its privacy policy.

6. Controlling and deleting your data

  • Reset your progress: in the Game, open Settings → Reset progress.
  • Delete all of the Game's data from your device: uninstall the Game.
  • Limit interest-based ads or reset your advertising identifier: in your Android settings (Privacy, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Requests about data processed by YANDEX LLC can be sent through the contacts in Yandex's privacy policy.
  • For any questions about this policy, write to the Developer: lungugamedev@gmail.com

7. Children

The Game is meant for users aged 12 and over and is not directed at younger children.

8. Changes

The Developer may update this policy. A new version is published in the Game and on its store page, with its effective date.
